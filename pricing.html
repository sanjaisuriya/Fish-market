Pricing & Packaging FAQ

Clear answers about weight measurements, custom cutting fees, and delivery policies.

We weigh every whole fish before descaling and gutting (gross catch weight), and our package labels state both the gross catch weight and net yield weight. All our curated value boxes are priced on guaranteed net cleaned weight so you receive exact edible meat portions without waste.

No extra charge! All UV-treated water descaling, gutting, gill trimming, curry cut cubing, steak slicing, and sashimi filleting are 100% complimentary on every order.

All orders of ₹500 and above receive completely free express same-day delivery in our 0°–4°C temperature-controlled refrigerated vans. Orders below ₹500 have a nominal delivery fee of ₹49.

Registered restaurants and catering partners qualify for our wholesale rate sheet, early 6:00 AM priority dock delivery, and weekly Net-30 credit invoicing after a quick trade verification with our dock manager.
