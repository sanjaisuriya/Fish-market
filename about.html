Pioneering Cold-Chain Freshness

Our Story: From Harbor Dock to Culinary Excellence

Founded in 2012 by a 3rd generation coastal fisherman, Fish Market was born with a single mission: to eliminate stale supermarket fish and bring the pure, unadulterated sweetness of the morning ocean directly to seafood lovers.

We operate right on Harbor Bay Pier 44, greeting local registered artisanal boats at 5:00 AM every morning. By cutting out brokers and multi-day storage yards, our catch goes from coastal nets through medical-grade RO purification straight to your pan in under 3 hours.

The Pillars of Our Craft

Our Core Values

What sets Fish Market apart is our uncompromising adherence to 6 foundational standards.

1. Freshness

Harvested daily. Kept strictly on pure food-grade ice with zero chemical additives or artificial glazes.

2. Uncompromising Quality

Hand-selected grade-A fish with bright convex eyes, deep red gills, and resilient spring-back flesh.

3. Absolute Transparency

Full origin traceability for every catch, including landing dock location, vessel name, and date.

4. Ocean Sustainability

We strictly avoid juvenile fish, observe spawning bans, and partner exclusively with non-destructive fisheries.

5. Customer Satisfaction

Custom knife filleting to your exact culinary specifications backed by our dedicated freshness assurance.

6. Cold-Chain Integrity

Continuous chilled handling and insulated cold packaging from harbor dock landing to your doorstep.
