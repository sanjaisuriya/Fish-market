# Fish Market - Seafood & Fresh Fish Market Website Template

A modern, premium, fully responsive **Fish Market** website template crafted with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript ES6+, and Bootstrap 5.

Designed specifically for fresh seafood businesses, harbor fishmongers, artisanal fisheries, seafood delivery platforms, and seafood restaurants.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **18 Complete HTML Pages**:
  1. `index.html` - Home Page 1 (General Seafood Landing)
  2. `home-2.html` - Home Page 2 (Fresh Seafood Market Focus)
  3. `about.html` - About Us, Story, Mission, Values, Team & Timeline
  4. `products.html` - Interactive Products Catalog (Live Search, Category & Price Filters, Grid/List)
  5. `product-details.html` - In-depth Product View (Cutting Selector, Nutrition, FAQs, Order Enquiry)
  6. `services.html` - 8 Dedicated Market Services
  7. `cleaning-delivery.html` - 5-Step Cleaning Process, Cutting Styles & Cold-Chain Logistics
  8. `recipes.html` - Recipe Catalog with Search, Category & Difficulty Filters
  9. `recipe-details.html` - Recipe Detail with Ingredients Checklist, Print & Share
  10. `blog.html` - Knowledge Articles with Featured Guide & Pagination
  11. `blog-details.html` - Full Article with Sidebar, Categories & Social Sharing
  12. `pricing.html` - Curated Seafood Boxes & Comparison Matrix
  13. `contact.html` - Live Order Enquiry Form with Validation & Map
  14. `login.html` - Authentication Portal with Password Toggle
  15. `register.html` - Customer Sign-up with Real-time Validation
  16. `404.html` - Seafood Themed 404 Error Page
  17. `coming-soon.html` - Launch Page with Live JS Countdown Timer
  18. `maintenance.html` - Scheduled Pier Maintenance Notice

- **🌓 Dark & Light Mode**: Persisted in `localStorage` with contrast-optimized oceanic dark palettes.
- **🔄 RTL & LTR Support**: Complete bi-directional layout support for Arabic and Hebrew readers.
- **⚡ Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript**:
  - Live search and multi-criteria product filtering.
  - Price slider and sorting (price, name, ratings).
  - Grid vs. List view switcher.
  - Interactive Quick View modal.
  - Live recipe search, difficulty filter, and Web Share/Print API.
  - Interactive cutting style selector.
  - Real-time form validation with non-reload submissions and toast alerts.
  - Working live countdown timer.
  - Sticky navbar on scroll and back-to-top button.
- **📱 100% Fully Responsive**: Pixel-perfect at 320px, 375px, 425px, 768px, 1024px, 1280px, and 1440px+.
