Sterile Processing Protocol

Our 5-Step Cleaning Process

We eliminate the mess, odor, and hassle of traditional fish markets with cleanroom processing.

1
1. Catch Selection

Harvested from harbor boats and hand-sorted on ice beds within 30 minutes of landing.

2
2. Inspection

3-point check: eye clarity, gill coloration, and flesh elasticity testing.

3
3. RO Water Wash

Thoroughly rinsed in UV-purified RO water at 2°C to remove scales and natural residues.

4
4. Precision Cutting

Cut to your exact culinary specifications using sterilized surgical steel knives.

5
5. Vacuum Seal

Hermetically sealed in leak-proof food trays packed with dry gel ice blankets.

Culinary Customization

Master Cutting Options

Select the cut that fits your recipe. We prepare every order right before dispatch.

Whole Scaled & Gutted

Head and tail intact, descaled, gills and innards completely cleared. Perfect for whole oven baking, salt crusting, or charcoal barbecuing.

Best for: Whole Roasts & Tandoor
Curry Cut (Bone-In Cubes)

Evenly portioned bone-in chunks that retain natural juices and enrich traditional coastal curries and broths.

Best for: Coconut Stews & Curries
Fry Cut (Slit Incisions)

Diagonal incisions along the body allow spice marinades to penetrate deep into the flesh for crispy shallow frying.

Best for: Tawa Fry & Crispy Semolina
Boneless Skin-On Fillet

Pin-bones removed, pristine skin intact for a crispy sear in butter, olive oil, or delicate citrus reductions.

Best for: Pan-Searing & Steaming
Steak Cut (Cross-Sections)

Thick circular cuts through the central bone (ideal for Salmon, King Mackerel, Surmai, and Tuna).

Best for: Grilling & Fish Steaks
Custom Chef Cut

Butterfly tail, sashimi blocks, headless deveined prawns, or cracked crab claws. Specify during order checkout.

Best for: Gourmet Chefs

Cleaning & Delivery FAQs

Clear answers to your most common questions about hygiene, weight calculations, and storage.

Gross weight is measured whole before cleaning. During standard descaling, gutting, and gill removal, fish typically lose approximately 15% to 20% in wastage. When ordering boneless fillets, skin-off wastage is around 40%-50%. We always display both gross and net estimates transparently.

Because our seafood is delivered fresh and packed ice-cold, it can be safely stored in the coldest compartment of your refrigerator (0°C to 4°C) for up to 48 hours in its vacuum tray. For longer storage, freeze immediately in an airtight freezer wrap.

Yes! Prawns can be ordered whole shell-on, headless shell-on, or completely peeled and deveined with tail-on. Live blue crabs are scrubbed, halved, and their claws gently cracked upon request so they are ready for cooking.

Our temperature-shielded thermal containers and dry ice blankets keep the seafood safely chilled at 2°C for up to 6 hours even if left at your doorstep under shade. Our driver will send you a photo confirmation upon drop-off.
