Herb & Lemon Garlic Grilled Salmon

Created by Master Chef Marcus Sterling
15 Mins
Prep Time
12 Mins
Cook Time
4 Servings
Servings
Easy
Difficulty

About This Culinary Creation

This classic Mediterranean recipe brings out the deep natural richness of Norwegian Atlantic Salmon. Basted with an emulsion of cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil, minced garlic cloves, fresh rosemary sprigs, and lemon zest, the salmon stays succulent on the inside with an exquisite charred crust on the outside.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Preheat & Prep the Salmon

Preheat your outdoor grill or heavy cast-iron skillet to medium-high heat (approx. 200°C / 400°F). Pat the fresh salmon fillets thoroughly dry with paper towels to ensure crispy skin.

2
Whisk the Herb Marinade

In a bowl, combine 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, 3 crushed garlic cloves, 1 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary, 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves, lemon zest, 1 tsp sea salt, and freshly ground black pepper.

3
Season & Brush

Brush both sides of the fillets generously with the herb-garlic mixture. Lightly brush the grill grates with vegetable oil to prevent sticking.

4
Sear to Perfection

Place salmon flesh-side down on the hot grill. Cook undisturbed for 5-6 minutes until grill marks appear. Carefully flip skin-side down and grill for an additional 4-5 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 52°C (125°F) for medium.

5
Garnish & Serve Warm

Transfer to a warm platter. Squeeze fresh charred lemon juice over each fillet, sprinkle with micro parsley, and serve immediately alongside roasted asparagus or garlic butter baby potatoes.
