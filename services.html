How Our Express Delivery & Butchery Works

From harbor nets to doorstep within 3 hours — maximum ocean freshness with sterile handling.

Step 1
5:00 AM Dock Intake

Direct intake from local day-boats. Chilled immediately in sterilized seawater ice flakes.

Step 2
Ozone Wash & Clean

Washed in RO purified water, descaled, degutted, and trimmed with zero chemical additives.

Step 3
Custom Chef Butchery

Portioned into steaks, fillets, curry cubes, or whole fish per your exact recipe instructions.

Step 4
Chilled Doorstep Drop

Sealed in food-grade thermal boxes with ice packs and delivered in refrigerated vans.
