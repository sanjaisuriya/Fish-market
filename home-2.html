Hygiene Protocol

Our 5-Step Clean & Cut Standard

We sanitize every catch in medical-grade chilled RO washrooms with customized chef cutting styles.

1. Dockside Inspection

Checked for clear glossy eyes, bright red gills, firm meat, and natural sea fragrance.

2. RO Water Descaling

Washed in pure UV-filtered RO water at 2°C to eliminate dock residues and microbes.

3. Master Cutting

Filleted, steaked, curry cut, or left whole according to your cooking preferences.

4. Vacuum Chill Seal

Hermetically sealed in multi-layer oxygen-barrier food-grade vacuum trays.

5. Insulated Cold Delivery

Delivered in temperature-monitored cooler boxes packed with non-melting ice gel pads.
