Learn & Explore

Seafood Knowledge Corner

Helpful tips and guidance to make the best seafood choices for your recipes.

Fish vs. Shellfish

Ocean fish provide delicate flaky textures ideal for searing or baking, while crustaceans like prawns and crabs offer dense, sweet brininess.

Popular Fish Cuts

Understand the difference between boneless loins for sashimi, center-cut steaks for grilling, and curry cubes for simmered dishes.

Whole Fish vs. Fillet

Whole fish retains deep juiciness during oven roasting, while fillets offer bone-free convenience for rapid pan frying.

How to Spot Fresh Seafood

Look for clear bulging eyes, bright red gills, firm springy flesh that bounces back, and a mild, fresh ocean aroma.

Storage & Preservation Tips

Store raw fish over an ice bed inside your refrigerator at 0°C to 2°C. Wrap tightly to preserve natural moisture and prevent drying.

Cooking Techniques Masterclass

Pan-sear fillets skin-side down for crispy texture, steam whole fish with ginger and soy, or simmer steaks in rich tamarind curries.
